## Implemented but not live-verified

- **Ollama.** Not installed/reachable in this environment; the 7 live Ollama tests
  were collected and skipped. Unverified live: real prompt-token counts vs the
  2-chars/token estimate (measured offline with the Qwen2.5 tokenizer only), whether
  Ollama's `prompt_eval_count` excludes cached prefix tokens (weakening the
  server-side overflow check; the pre-flight estimate does not depend on it), and
  real-model verdict quality.
- **Wazuh.** NOT VERIFIED live. The `order` sort field and two-sided queries are
  unit-tested against the query builder and mock transports only.

## Known limitations

- The benign requirements are necessary, not sufficient: a genuinely abused
  management channel whose descendants stay inside the retrieval window's blind spots
  (e.g. activity > the network window after the alert, or not in retained telemetry)
  cannot be distinguished. Signer/hash enrichment is still absent.
- Descendant walk bounds (8 generations, 25 processes per tool call) are truncation,
  which blocks benign on very busy trees by design.
- Only host-wide network queries satisfy the network requirement; very busy hosts will
  be `insufficient_evidence` more often (safe, but costly).
- Compaction thresholds (130 chars; entropy 3.5/4.0 bits) are heuristics; shorter
  encoded strings stay verbatim (their token cost is bounded by the 2.0 ratio).
- Fixture matching is case-insensitive while Wazuh `term` queries are exact (F8, unchanged).
- The benchmark is 22 synthetic cases written by the same reviewer as the guards; it
  is a regression suite, not an accuracy measurement.
